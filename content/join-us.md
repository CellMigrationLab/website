# Join us

We are always happy to hear from motivated people who want to study how cells move, and to build
the microscopy and image-analysis tools to do it.

<p class="cm-lead">We have no funded positions open at the moment. We welcome applications for
fellowships with the Cell Migration Lab as host.</p>

## Postdoctoral researchers

Postdocs join us with their own fellowship. We help you choose a scheme, shape the project and write
the application. The main options are:

- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships) (European Commission)
- [EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.embo.org/funding/fellowships-grants-and-career-support/postdoctoral-fellowships/)
- [Human Frontier Science Program fellowships](https://www.hfsp.org/funding/hfsp-funding/postdoctoral-fellowships)
- [Research Council of Finland](https://www.aka.fi/en/research-funding/) postdoctoral funding
- Finnish foundations, such as the [Sigrid Jusélius Foundation](https://www.sigridjuselius.fi/en/)
  and the [Finnish Cultural Foundation](https://skr.fi/en)

## PhD students

Doctoral researchers at Åbo Akademi University are admitted through its
[doctoral education](https://www.abo.fi/en/research/doctoral-education/), in our case the Doctoral
Programme in Biosciences and Drug Research. Positions are advertised there when funding is available;
you can also apply with your own funding.

## Master's students and interns

Master's students can do their thesis project with us, for example within the
[Master's Degree Programme in Biomedical Imaging](https://www.abo.fi/en/degree-programme/masters-degree-programme-in-biomedical-imaging/)
at Åbo Akademi University and the University of Turku. Students from abroad can come for a research
internship, for example with an
[Erasmus+ traineeship](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/opportunities-for-individuals/students/traineeships-abroad-for-students).

## How to get in touch

Email [Guillaume Jacquemet](mailto:guillaume.jacquemet@abo.fi) with:

1. your CV and a list of publications (if any);
2. a few lines on what you would like to work on, and why in our lab;
3. the fellowship or programme you plan to apply to, and its deadline;
4. the names of two referees.

For fellowships, please write **at least two to three months before the deadline**, so that we
have time to develop the project together.

Read about our [research](research.md), our [papers](publications.md) and the
[people in the lab](lab-members.md) to see whether we are a good fit.
