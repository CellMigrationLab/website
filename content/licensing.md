# Licensing and image credits

**Website code.** The code behind this website (scripts, templates and style sheets) is
open source under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/CellMigrationLab/website/blob/main/LICENSE).

**Images and other media are not covered by the MIT licence.** Each image is credited next
to it, and its credit says what you may do with it:

- **"© … · CC BY 4.0"**: you may reuse and adapt the image, also commercially, under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
  credit the people named and link to the licence.
- **"© …" with no licence**: all rights reserved by the people or the lab named; please ask
  before reusing it.
- **No credit**: its rights are not yet established; please ask before reusing it.

**Third-party material** – journal covers, publisher figures, and the logos of universities,
funders and software projects – belongs to its owners and keeps their terms.

To ask about using an image, or if you think one is credited wrongly, contact
[Guillaume Jacquemet](about-us.md#group-leader).

**Visit statistics.** This website counts visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/),
a privacy-friendly service: it sets no cookies and stores no personal data. It records which
pages are read, from which country and from which website a visitor arrived, so that the
lab can see how the site is used.
